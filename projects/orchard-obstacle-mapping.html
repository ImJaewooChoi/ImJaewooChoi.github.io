Obstacle mapping

Daedong AILab, Senior AI Researcher, May 2024–Present.

Problem

Orchard robots have to recognize open‑set obstacles and turn them into a navigable map under embedded compute limits.

My role

I pre‑trained the segmentation stack, adapted it to orchards, and built the occupancy‑grid mapping.

Method

  1. I pre‑trained the adapter, neck, and segmentation head on multiple datasets over frozen DINOv2 and SigLIP2 encoders, with region‑text alignment for instance and semantic segmentation.
  2. The model takes text prompts at inference and can run with a fixed vocabulary. I adapted it to the orchard domain.
  3. Point‑cloud voxel centers are projected into the image and tied to instance masks. Virtual voxels are added from valid anchor depths. Obstacles go into an occupancy grid with decay, hysteresis, and cost inflation.

Result

The pipeline runs on production RT100 platforms and is optimized with TensorRT on Jetson Nano.

Stack

DINOv2, SigLIP2, TensorRT, and Jetson Nano.
