Auto‑labeling pipeline

Daedong AILab, Senior AI Researcher, May 2024–Present.

Problem

Manual annotation cost limits the supply of segmentation data and region‑grounded vision‑language training data.

My role

I built the video auto‑labeling pipeline, set the label taxonomy and the annotation guidelines, and compiled the COCO‑format training datasets.

Method

  1. Point prompts and mask corrections become tracked masks with consistent IDs across frames.
  2. Image and video inputs share TensorRT engines, so the same computation is not duplicated.
  3. Qwen‑series vision‑language models write region‑grounded object descriptions and check them. Uncertain cases go to a second review. Verified descriptions are linked to the instance annotations.

Result

Per‑image labeling cost fell from about USD 1.05 to about USD 0.036, a 96.6% reduction.

Stack

TensorRT, Qwen‑series vision‑language models, and COCO.
