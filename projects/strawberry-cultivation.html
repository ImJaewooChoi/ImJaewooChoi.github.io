Strawberry cultivation tasks

Daedong AILab, Senior AI Researcher, May 2024–Present.

Problem

Transplanting, flower thinning, and runner handling are learned from human demonstrations collected with a Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI).

My role

I led the data pipeline and the policy training for all three tasks. I built everything except the robot‑side post‑processing.

Method

  1. I collected GoPro RGB and IMU data and calibrated the camera to the IMU.
  2. I turned ORB‑SLAM3 visual‑inertial SLAM trajectories into training sets.
  3. I trained Diffusion Policy with RGB features and robot state as global conditioning, and compared U‑Net and ViT configurations.

Result

Across 1,000 trials in a fixed lab, transplanting succeeded 93.8% of the time, flower thinning 87.5%, and runner handling 65%. The work was exhibited at CES 2025.

Stack

UMI, GoPro, IMU, ORB‑SLAM3, Diffusion Policy, U‑Net, and ViT.
