Strawberry cultivation tasks

Daedong AILab, Senior AI Researcher, May 2024–Present.

Problem

A strawberry cultivation robot has to transplant seedlings, thin flowers, and handle runners. Each task policy is learned from human demonstrations collected with a Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI).

My role

I led the data pipeline and the policy training for all three tasks, and built everything except the robot‑side post‑processing.

Method

  1. GoPro RGB and IMU data were collected, and I calibrated the camera to the IMU.
  2. ORB‑SLAM3 visual‑inertial SLAM trajectories were turned into training sets.
  3. Diffusion Policy was trained with RGB features and robot state as global conditioning.

Model structure

The policy is Diffusion Policy. An image encoder turns the RGB frames into features. Those features and the robot state form the global conditioning. Starting from a noisy action sequence, the denoising network removes noise over repeated steps and returns an action sequence for the robot side. I compared U‑Net and ViT configurations.

Result

The three task policies run on the cultivation robot. Task‑level success rates measured over 1,000 work cycles in a fixed lab environment were 93.8% for transplanting, 87.5% for flower thinning, and 65% for runner handling. The work was exhibited at CES 2025.
