CAD‑based bin picking

PLAIF, Computer Vision Engineer, Jan. 2023–Apr. 2024.

Problem

Picking poses for objects with known CAD geometry come from RGB‑D images of a bin.

My role

I trained the segmenter and aligned visible‑surface CAD templates to the segmented points.

Method

  1. I trained Mask R‑CNN (ResNet‑101) with separate unoccluded and hole classes, subtracted hole masks from object masks, and segmented the point cloud with the remaining instance masks.
  2. I built visible‑surface CAD templates for each pick pose by hidden‑point removal, and I aligned them to the segmented points with PCA initialization and point‑to‑point ICP.
  3. I checked each fit model‑to‑scene and scene‑to‑model, filtered poses by registration fitness, inlier RMSE, and a combined score, then ranked the candidates and transformed the poses into the robot base with the camera extrinsics.

Model architecture

Result

In 1,000 in‑house, fixed‑environment trials, the robot moved five object types into an empty container with 89% task success.

Stack

Mask R‑CNN, ResNet‑101, PCA, and point‑to‑point ICP.
