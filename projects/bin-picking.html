CAD‑based bin picking

PLAIF, Computer Vision Engineer, Jan. 2023–Apr. 2024.

Problem

Picking poses for objects with known CAD geometry come from RGB‑D images of a bin.

My role

I trained the segmenter and aligned visible‑surface CAD templates to the segmented points.

Method

  1. The instance segmentation model was trained with separate unoccluded and hole classes. Hole masks were subtracted from object masks, and the remaining instance masks segmented the point cloud.
  2. For each pick pose, I built a visible‑surface CAD template by hidden‑point removal and aligned it to the segmented points with PCA initialization and point‑to‑point ICP.
  3. Poses were filtered by registration fitness and inlier RMSE, then ranked and transformed into the robot base with the camera extrinsics.

Result

In 1,000 in‑house, fixed‑environment trials, the robot moved five object types into an empty container with 89% task success.
