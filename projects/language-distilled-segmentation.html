Open‑vocabulary segmentation

Daedong AILab, Senior AI Researcher, May 2024–Present.

Problem

RT100, an autonomous orchard platform, needs instance masks for objects outside a fixed label set. A language teacher is available during training. The deployed model does not add inference cost.

My role

I adapted a DETR‑based segmenter for the RT100 orchard platform.

Method

  1. The backbone is a frozen DINOv3, and a lightweight text encoder trains.
  2. A multi‑scale feature projector feeds a three‑layer decoder. The decoder predicts masks from dot products between queries and refined spatial features.
  3. During training only, object queries and pooled image features are matched to precomputed language‑teacher embeddings of object and image descriptions. Semantically related negatives are reweighted so they are not pushed apart.

Result

Instance masks feed point‑cloud projection and occupancy‑grid mapping. The model runs in the production RT100 perception pipeline, and the language teacher is used only during training.

Stack

DETR, a frozen DINOv3 backbone, a lightweight text encoder, a multi‑scale feature projector, and a three‑layer decoder.
