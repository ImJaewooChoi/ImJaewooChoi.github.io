Open‑vocabulary segmentation and mapping

Daedong AILab, Senior AI Researcher, May 2024–Present.

Problem

RT100, an autonomous orchard platform, needs instance masks for objects outside a fixed label set, and those masks have to become a navigable map under embedded compute limits. A language teacher can supervise the model during training without adding inference cost.

My role

I pre‑trained a DINOv3 and SigLIP2 segmenter (Version 1), implemented and trained a second open‑vocabulary segmenter (Version 2), and built the occupancy‑grid mapping from the instance masks.

Method

Version 1

With the DINOv3 and SigLIP2 encoders frozen, I pre‑trained the adapter, neck, and segmentation head on multiple datasets, with region‑text alignment for instance and semantic segmentation. On top of that model I added text‑prompt inference and fixed‑vocabulary conversion, and adapted it to the orchard domain.

Version 2

Version 2 is a DETR‑style instance segmentation model. Its frozen DINOv3 backbone feeds a multi‑scale projector and a three‑layer decoder. A trainable MiniLM text encoder embeds category names, and a query‑based mask head predicts masks from the highest‑resolution features. Training uses category names, mask captions, and image captions. During training only, a precomputed language teacher aligns the captions with matched queries and pooled image features and reweights related negatives. Masks are matched and trained with BCE/Dice losses.

Post‑processing

Point‑cloud voxel centers were projected into the image and associated with the instance masks. I added virtual voxels from valid anchor depths, and obstacles were mapped into a navigable occupancy grid with decay, hysteresis, and cost inflation.

Result

Version 1 is integrated into the production RT100 perception pipeline. I deployed it on production RT100 platforms and optimized it with TensorRT on Jetson Nano. For Version 2, the detection model exports to ONNX and TensorRT with parity checks against PyTorch and ONNX Runtime. Full‑dataset training and accuracy evaluation of Version 2 are pending.
