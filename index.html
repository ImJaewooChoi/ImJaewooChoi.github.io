I work on perception for orchard robots and for manipulation robots. Before Daedong AILab I was a computer vision engineer at PLAIF and at FS Solution.

Research interests

I work on perception for real robots: open-vocabulary RGB-D segmentation, point-cloud and image fusion for obstacle mapping, 6D pose estimation, and imitation learning for manipulation. I have developed and tested this work on deployed orchard robots and on manipulation robots. I want to extend it toward vision-language-action models that connect what a robot sees with how it acts.

Open-Vocabulary 3D Obstacle Perception and Occupancy Mapping for Autonomous Orchard Robots

Open-set obstacles are segmented and written into an occupancy grid. The pipeline runs on production RT100 robots with TensorRT on Jetson Nano.

3D perception, TensorRT, Jetson Nano

Auto-Labeling and Data Captioning System

Point prompts and mask corrections become tracked masks. Qwen-series models write region-grounded captions. Per-image labeling cost fell from about USD 1.05 to about USD 0.036.

Auto-labeling, TensorRT, Qwen

Strawberry Cultivation-Task Robot with UMI and Diffusion Policy

Diffusion Policy trained on UMI demonstrations. In a fixed lab, across 1,000 trials, success was 93.8% for transplanting, 87.5% for flower thinning, and 65% for runner handling. Exhibited at CES 2025.

Imitation learning, UMI, Diffusion Policy

CAD-free Auto Assembly

6D tool poses for assembly from a 2D camera, with no CAD model of the part. 93% task success across 1,000 in-house trials. Basis of Korean Patent 10-2694987.
