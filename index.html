Research interests

I work on perception for real robots: open‑vocabulary RGB‑D segmentation, point‑cloud and image fusion for obstacle mapping, 6D pose estimation, and imitation learning for manipulation. I have developed and tested this work on deployed orchard robots and on manipulation robots. I want to extend it toward vision‑language‑action models that connect what a robot sees with how it acts.
